# Synopsis - Momotarou Densetsu (PS1)

Built from a read of the extracted script (2026-10-30). Ids are `TABLE:index`. Translators: don't give away the late
reveals in section 4 in earlier lines.

## 1. Story, in play order
- **Opening (EVTOP_25):** the folk tale: a peach floats downriver ("Donburako, donburako" refrain), the baby bursts
  out (bare-bottomed kid humour, keep it). Oni from Onigashima torment people; Momotaro sets out. Goal: punish Great
  King Enma (えんまを こらしめる), whose oni plague the land.
- **Tabidachi Village** (旅立ちの村, home SMAP00 / village SMAP01): Grandma gives 100 ryo and millet dumplings,
  Grandpa straw sandals and cards for the Card Album. Poop (ウンチ, tic ぷり) tags along. Shrines save, the Jizo
  gives level hints.
- **Kachikachi Village** (SMAP02): rabbits and tanuki reconciled 300 years ago (Daimonji bonfire festival). Silver
  Oni on the mountain; beaten, he apologises.
- **Omusubi Village** (SMAP03): mice build a village in a cave; oni Ryotetsu stole their food; mouse Jinpachi joins.
- **Bombee Village** (SMAP04): poverty gods (tic なのねん); Gold Oni turned the village gold; chief Enon joins;
  Iron Poop; King Bombee's statue steals money (Momotetsu parody).
- **Mystery Spring** (SMAP05): the Poop Goddess's honest-woodcutter parody (gold/silver/iron poop).
- **Hanasaka Village** (SMAP06): withered cherry trees; a fake Old Man Hanasaka is Black Oni in disguise (twist
  inside this village). Dog joins for a millet dumpling.
- **Mystery Forest** (SMAP07): Monkey joins. Pheasant joins early too.
- **Kintaro Village** (SMAP08, DMAP02): festival; Ryutoki kidnapped the children into the Gold Cave; Kintaro joins
  (オラ, rustic, loud, cries easily); Ryutoki repents.
- **Urashima Village / Ryugu Palace** (SMAP09-10): oni bully the turtle, Tatsumaki Oni took Otohime; Urashima joins
  (polite です/ます); Dragon Necklace (1st of five treasures).
- **Shuten-doji's Riddle Mountain** (DMAP03): six floors of puzzles (shiritori, compass quiz, memory, code); Shuten
  (オレ, rival, "Remember the name Shuten-doji!") mocks courage, loses, finds real courage.
- **Netaro Village** (SMAP12): sleeper Netaro blocks the bridge; waking him needs the Icehouse Fire from Shikome
  (DMAP05). Ginji the Pickpocket steals treasures (later reformed, runs a restaurant).
- **Yashahime** (DMAP16, ice cave by the Ice Tower): "an oni of royal blood who commands snow and ice", hostile and
  haughty at first (お前, わ/よ, なくってよ); beaten, her face softens (BM000_1:181) and she asks to join; Kintaro
  and Urashima vouch for her; afterwards polite (-san).
- **Hohoemi Village** (SMAP13): Mashira & the Blue Monkeys force a visual-kei rock concert on pun-loving villagers;
  then a Pun Contest (readers' animal puns, real names and prefectures: keep names, rebuild the puns).
- **Tokonatsu Village** (SMAP15, DMAP04): Water Oni (水鬼) dried the Spring of Life; beach couple gags.
- **Capital of Hope** (SMAP16, HMAP20/26): darkened by Ashura (私, ふふふふふふっ, dandy, collects brides: on bride
  No. 1000). Yashahime poses as the bride, Momotaro hides in a sake barrel; Ashura recognises "Princess Yasha" and
  is beaten, spared, leaves to persuade Enma. Museum, costume rental, minigames, card/pun pagoda.
- **Fujin's Valley** (SMAP17): Fujin ("Fwahahaha!") sees that friendship is Momotaro's power and scatters the
  party; Momotaro wakes alone at Sparrow Inn 4 and regathers everyone.
- **Shiobuki Village** (SMAP20): Salt Oni and the salt mill bury the village (しお！しお！); pickle-shop rivals wed.
- **Usakame Village** (SMAP21): Fujin rematch; he repents and goes to persuade Enma. Horai Jewel (treasure).
- **Hikoboshi / Orihime** (SMAP22, EVT069 Tanabata): men and women split 100 years ago; Gozu and Mezu (echoing
  pairs) block the road; Orihime's flute summons the boat.
- **Taketori Village** (SMAP23): Princess Kaguya was taken to Onigashima; her Sword of Courage is hidden in glowing
  bamboo and needs all five treasures (Dragon Necklace, Buddha's Bowl, Golden Fur, Horai Jewel, Swallow's Cowry).
- **Raijin** (DMAP11): sermon on oni hierarchy, recants; Rashomon (Yomi tower general) also converts. The Konki
  twins guard the gate of Hell (keys from the Left/Right Soul Towers).
- **Onigashima / Hell** (EVT076, DMAP15): the sword opens the sea path (fails if friends are missing). Enma
  questions Momotaro on Courage, Hope, Justice and Love, loses, admits his error; he had Kaguya sent home. Shuten,
  Ashura, Fujin, Raijin, Rashomon say goodbye.
- **Endings:** END00 (the Moon Crystal, "proof of friendship between humans and oni"), END02 farewell in Urashima's
  village then the staff roll (real names, jokes), SMAP26 Yashahime returns to Hell for her royal duty, END01 home:
  "...and they lived happily ever after."

## 3. Running gags to keep
- **Mangled proverbs:** hermit huts and village walls show 「ことわざが 書いてある！」, a twisted proverb, then
  "そな アホな！" ("Oh, come on!"). Twist a real English idiom the same way ("Rome wasn't built in a day" ->
  something silly), keep the reader's groan. The pagoda (HMAP26_66) has ~60 more; HMAP2606_65 lists the real ones.
- **Pun contests** (SMAP13, ATOK_9 comedians Ari to Kirigirisu): rebuild every animal pun in English.
- Grandpa's dance and slipped disc ("Hey hey, c'mon! ...Crick! My back!"), poop jokes, the cow's healing milk,
  Momotetsu parody (Bombee, King Bombee), sentai Oni Rangers, visual-kei band, Dragon Ball senzu beans, gourmet /
  literature / kanji quiz oni (quiz answers name real people: keep names; kanji quizzes need an English redesign
  that keeps the right answer's position).
- Riddles that depend on Japanese (reversed-text riddle WMAP20_28:6, shiritori DMAP03_92:11-19, tongue twister):
  make an English puzzle with the same answer.

## 4. Late reveals (don't leak early)
- Yashahime is cold and hostile before her defeat; no -san or friendship from her before DMAP16.
- Enma, Ashura, Shuten, Fujin, Raijin all repent later: early oni lines are hostile but not irredeemably evil.
- Ashura's bride trap works until he recognises Yashahime.
- Kaguya's sword and the five treasures are explained at Taketori; Enma sent Kaguya home (Hell scene).
- Black Oni disguised as Old Man Hanasaka is revealed inside Hanasaka Village.
